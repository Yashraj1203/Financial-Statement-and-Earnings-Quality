- FY26 EBITDA: ₹7,419.7 crore — sourced from consolidated Ten-Year Review.
- FY26 PAT: ₹4,325.3 crore — sourced from consolidated Ten-Year Review.
- FY26 net CFO: ₹7,088.18 crore — sourced from consolidated cash-flow statement.
- FY26 inventory: ₹5,743.69 crore — sourced from consolidated balance sheet.
- FY26 net trade receivables: ₹4,461.80 crore — sourced from consolidated balance sheet.
- FY26 trade payables: ₹3,863.04 crore — sourced from consolidated balance sheet.


## Independent calculations
- FY26 revenue growth: 5.09%
- FY26 EBITDA growth: 12.78%
- FY26 PAT growth: 17.95%
- FY26 CFO/PAT: 1.64x


## Analytical safeguards
- Consolidated and standalone figures are not mixed in the core historical series.
- FY25 restatements are accepted as presented in the FY26 Ten-Year Review.
- Earnings-quality indicators are presented as diagnostics rather than definitive judgments.
- FY26 working-capital interpretation explicitly separates inventory, receivables and payables effects.


## Important limitation
The model is designed for research demonstration, not statutory accounting. Some ratios use proxy denominators where exact COGS or average balance data is not available in the selected source set.
