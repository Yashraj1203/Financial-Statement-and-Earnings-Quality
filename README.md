# Asian Paints — Financial Statement & Earnings Quality Analysis

## Developer
[Yashraj1203](https://github.com/Yashraj1203)

### Research question
How durable and cash-backed are Asian Paints' reported earnings, and what do working capital, capital efficiency and balance-sheet changes reveal about underlying financial quality?

### Coverage
- Consolidated reported financials: FY22–FY26
- Detailed working-capital forensic analysis: FY25–FY26
- Earnings-quality diagnostics: CFO/PAT, EBITDA-to-CFO, FCF/PAT where comparable data is        available, accrual proxy
- Capital efficiency: ROCE / RONW and balance-sheet leverage
- FY26 receivables ageing / ECL monitoring

### Important source convention
The primary historical series uses Asian Paints' consolidated Ten-Year Review. The company also publishes standalone metrics; these are not mixed into the consolidated historical series.

### FY26 headline reported figures
Revenue from sale of products/services: ₹35,516.4 crore
EBITDA: ₹7,419.7 crore
PAT: ₹4,325.3 crore
Net CFO: ₹7,088.2 crore
ROCE: 27.6%

### Key FY26 analytical observations
- Revenue grew 5.1% YoY while EBITDA grew 12.8% and PAT 17.9% on the consolidated ten-year-review basis.
- Net CFO increased sharply to ₹7,088.2 crore from ₹4,424.0 crore.
- Inventory decreased by ₹975.6 crore and trade payables increased by ₹396.1 crore, supporting operating cash flow; receivables absorbed ₹274.0 crore.
- Current trade receivables were ₹4,461.8 crore net of an ECL allowance; the ageing disclosure identifies credit-impaired balances that should be monitored.
- Consolidated ROCE recovered to 27.6% from 26.9% but remained below FY24's 39.7%.
